This repository contains all 21 C programs from my BCA 1st Semester practical file for viva preparation.

### 📚 List of Programs
1. Arithmetic Operations (+, -, *, /)
2. Find Remainder using Modulus Operator
3. Area & Perimeter of Rectangle
4. Area & Perimeter of Square
5. Area & Circumference of Circle
6. Volume of Cylinder
7. Swapping Two Numbers (with and without third variable)
8. Average of Two and Three Numbers
9. Simple Interest Calculation
10. Percentage and Average of Student Marks
11. Temperature Conversion (Celsius to Fahrenheit)
12. Unit Conversion (KM to Meter, Meter to CM)
13. Speed, Distance, Time Calculation

### 💻 Concepts Covered
- printf(), scanf()
- Data Types: int, float, char
- Operators: Arithmetic, Modulus
- Header file: stdio.h
